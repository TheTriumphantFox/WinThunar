# Code of conduct

WinThunar is a hobby-scale open-source project, and we want it to be a place where people can ask
questions, report bugs, and send patches without being treated badly.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and explain your reasoning when you push back on a change.
- Keep issues and reviews on topic and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone asking a basic question was once new to this.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Spam, or promotion unrelated to the project
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these
rules. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact the
maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.

This code applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space
where someone represents WinThunar.
